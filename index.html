Stremio ↔ Nuvio Live Sync

Free & stateless. Sync live playback position and watched-state between Stremio and Nuvio — one addon URL, installed in both apps.

1 Connect your Nuvio account

Your token is used only to talk to Nuvio’s API. Nothing is stored on any server.

The token lives in the URL path — that is the whole “login” system. Treat this URL like a password: it grants access to your Nuvio progress.

2 Install in your apps

The same URL works in Stremio and Nuvio — both speak the Stremio addon protocol.

Nuvio TV: Addons → Manage from phone → scan this QR code

Phones can also open this page and copy the URL.

  1. Stremio (Desktop / Web / Android / TV): open Addons → paste the URL above → Install. You should see a new Continue Watching (Nuvio) row.
  2. Nuvio (Android / TV / Desktop): Settings → Content & Discovery → Addons → paste the same URL into Add Addon → Install.
  3. Watch something in Stremio. Start, pause, seek and close events are pushed to your Nuvio account within seconds. Open Nuvio’s Continue Watching — the movie is there with its position.
  4. Watch something in Nuvio (if your client also emits player events), then open Stremio’s Continue Watching (Nuvio) row to resume.
How it works (and how to verify it is syncing)

The addon is a stateless Cloudflare Worker. It declares the Stremio protocol’s event resources, which is the part most addons never use: instead of serving content, the addon receives your activity.

Stremio sendsWorker does
/player/movie/tt0137523/action=start&currentTime=0&duration=7200000.json Parses action, currentTime (ms), duration (ms) from the path, then POSTs to sync_push_watch_progress.
.../action=pause&currentTime=1830000&duration=7200000.json Pushes position 1 830 000 ms (30:30) to Nuvio — that is your resume point.
.../action=stop&currentTime=6900000&duration=7200000.json Fires even when the player closes or the app exits abruptly. Pushes the final position; Nuvio auto-marks watched at ≥90%.
/library/movie/tt0137523/action=watched.json Pushes a watched-history item to Nuvio.
/catalog/movie/nuviocw.json (you opening Stremio) Pulls your Nuvio progress and renders the Continue Watching (Nuvio) row.

Verify it works: the addon operator can run npx wrangler tail (or open the Worker’s Logs in the Cloudflare dashboard) — every [player] line shows the parsed action, position and duration arriving live during playback.

Note: player events are a recent protocol addition. If your Stremio build does not send them yet, you will see no [player] lines in the logs — in that case only the catalog row (Nuvio→Stremio resume) and watched toggles sync. Updating Stremio/Nuvio to the latest version is the fix.

Privacy: no server stores anything. Your token is read from the URL on each request, used for one call to Nuvio’s API, and discarded. You can revoke access instantly by regenerating your token or uninstalling the addon in both apps.
