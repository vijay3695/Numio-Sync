How it works

The addon is a stateless Cloudflare Worker. It speaks the Stremio addon protocol and uses your two accounts as its only “database” — reading and writing each side directly through their own APIs.

WhenWhat happens
You open either app The app fetches the manifest; the Worker syncs both directions immediately — no need to open any particular row.
/player/... events Start, pause, seek and stop (including abrupt app exit) carry the exact position in ms and are pushed to Nuvio and written into your Stremio library.
Stremio → Nuvio Positions are read from your Stremio account (libraryItem.state.timeOffset) and pushed to Nuvio — this works even if your Stremio build never emits player events.
Nuvio → Stremio Progress is written into your Stremio account as library items with state.timeOffset, which is what Stremio's own Continue Watching reads to resume.
Rows in Stremio Continue Watching (Nuvio) and Watched (Nuvio) are populated from your Nuvio account; titles and posters come from your own libraries.
Watched marks Marking watched in either app writes to the other's history.

Privacy: nothing is stored anywhere — no database, no accounts, no analytics, no logs of your credentials or tokens. Every request reads the credentials from its own URL, uses them for one call to Nuvio's or Stremio's API, and discards them. Revoke access instantly by uninstalling the addon in both apps.

Advanced: paste a token instead

If you already have a Nuvio access token, use it directly instead of signing in.
